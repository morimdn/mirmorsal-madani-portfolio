Product Overview

A native mobile CRM application connected to the TopFast brokerage ecosystem. It brings account access, quick actions and analytics into a mobile experience for Trader, IB and ProTrader contexts.

Business Problem

Brokerage customers need to reach important account and relationship workflows on mobile. The product challenge is to make those actions understandable within a smaller interface and across different user roles.

Target Users

Traders, introducing brokers and ProTraders accessing their brokerage account and activity on mobile.

My Role & Ownership

Alongside my CPO responsibilities, I remain personally hands-on as Product Manager of the native CRM. I shape scope, workflows and UX and work closely with a Flutter developer through implementation and iteration.

Product Discovery & Strategy

My product work focuses on selecting the mobile scope and translating brokerage needs into clear user flows. Role-specific access and quick actions provide the organizing structure for the experience.

Key Product Decisions & Capabilities

The design separates Trader, IB and ProTrader contexts while bringing account access, requests, history and analytics into the same mobile journey. It also shows entry points for social trading and AI-assisted analysis.

Delivery & Cross-functional Collaboration

I work directly with the Flutter developer on scope, UX, implementation and iteration. This keeps product decisions close to the build process while maintaining a distinct mobile workstream from the desktop brokerage CRM.

Outcomes & Product Value

The application extends brokerage account and relationship workflows into a native mobile product. My continuing involvement connects product scope and user experience with day-to-day development decisions.
