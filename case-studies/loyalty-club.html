Product Overview

A brokerage loyalty platform connecting customer behavior with missions, progression and rewards. Customer-facing journeys sit alongside administration tools for configuring quests and reviewing activity.

Business Problem

Retention teams need a consistent way to connect customer activity with relevant incentives. The product makes segmentation, mission rules and reward eligibility part of a managed engagement workflow.

Target Users

Brokerage customers taking part in missions, and retention, marketing and operations teams managing segments, quests and rewards.

My Role & Ownership

As CPO, I lead product strategy, roadmaps and prioritization for Loyalty Club with Product Owners, designers and engineering teams. My product-management focus is the relationship between behavioral segmentation, quest progression and retention-focused workflows.

Product Discovery & Strategy

The product direction connects behavior signals to customer segments, quest packs and governed rewards. I work across product and business stakeholders to align priorities with engagement goals and the teams responsible for delivering them.

Key Product Decisions & Capabilities

Customers can browse missions, follow progress and claim eligible rewards. Administrators configure activation and completion conditions, review client progression and inspect activity history. Levels and reward rules give the experience a clear progression structure.

Delivery & Cross-functional Collaboration

I coordinate product direction and prioritization across Product Owners, product designers and cross-functional squads. The customer journey and administration tools are considered together so that engagement ideas can be operated by brokerage teams.

Outcomes & Product Value

Loyalty Club provides a structured way to run behavior-based engagement and retention programs. Segments, quests and reward governance give brokerage teams tools to connect incentives with customer activity and review program performance.
