Product overview

A risk-management product area focused on exposure, trading behavior, operational visibility and analytical reporting. The supplied UI artifacts carry the Riskotik name.

Business problem

Trading and business teams need a shared view of exposure and customer behavior to support risk decisions. The supplied presentation describes risk and exposure insights, monitoring, behavior analysis and operational alerts.

Target users

Brokerage risk, trading, operations and business teams. The dashboards show broker-level summaries, live orders, currency exposure and geographic profiles.

My product role

At DeltaFX, Product Manager / Business Intelligence, August 2020–March 2023: owned a risk-management product from problem definition and business requirements through prioritization and implementation. At Top Fast Technology, risk management is also part of my CPO portfolio. The relationship between the earlier DeltaFX implementation and these Riskotik designs is pending confirmation.

Product strategy and discovery

The CV supports problem definition, requirements, prioritization and collaboration with trading, engineering and business teams. It also describes SQL Server, Python, data analysis and customer segmentation to investigate trading and customer behavior.

Main capabilities

Exposure analysis by currency and geography; behavioral and pattern monitoring; live-order and risk indicators; operational reporting. Capabilities are supported by the supplied presentation and artifact labels, not by claims about detection accuracy.

Key workflows

Review broker-level indicators, inspect live orders and exposure, examine geographic risk profiles, and open detailed analytical reports. Account names and financial data are redacted in the gallery.

Delivery and technical collaboration

The CV documents technical implementation across trading, engineering and business teams and BI reporting/data modeling using SQL Server and Python. The Riskotik application architecture and implementation dates are not supplied.

Outcomes supported by evidence

Risk-product ownership and BI responsibilities are supported by the CV; the Riskotik artifacts document the UI and reporting workflows. Risk reduction, trading performance, client counts and product-specific rollout evidence are pending.
