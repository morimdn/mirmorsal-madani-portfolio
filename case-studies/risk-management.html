Product Overview

Riskotik is a brokerage risk management platform designed to provide deeper visibility into trading exposure, client behavior, and operational risk through advanced analytics and monitoring capabilities.

My Role & Contributions

I initiated the product concept and took ownership of its development journey, from early ideation and requirements definition to product design, prioritization, and implementation.

As Product Owner, I worked closely with stakeholders, product designers, and engineering teams to translate complex brokerage risk-management needs into practical workflows and product capabilities.

Key Contributions

  • Product ideation, discovery, and requirements engineering
  • Product roadmap, feature definition, and backlog prioritization
  • UX and workflow design collaboration
  • Cross-functional development coordination
  • Risk analytics and reporting requirements
  • Product delivery and continuous improvement

Product Capabilities

  • Trading exposure analysis
  • Risk monitoring dashboards
  • Customer behavior and trading-pattern analysis
  • Geographic and currency-level risk insights
  • Operational reports and decision-support tools
