Product Overview

Trading plugins within the TopFast brokerage ecosystem. The MT5 Dealing Desk Plugin provides execution-management controls alongside the broader MT4/MT5 product context.

Business Problem

Brokerage dealing teams need to manage order behavior and execution rules while maintaining operational continuity.

Target Users

Brokerage dealing, trading operations and technical teams managing MetaTrader environments.

My Role & Ownership

I lead product strategy, roadmaps and prioritization across TopFast’s trading-plugin work, coordinating Product Owners and technical teams around brokerage needs.

Product Discovery & Strategy

Product direction focuses on controllable execution behavior and practical operational workflows. Roadmap work connects brokerage requirements with the engineering teams responsible for the plugins.

Key Product Decisions & Capabilities

The MT5 Dealing Desk Plugin covers order rules, trade filtering, slippage, delay, strategy detection and execution management. Its design allows rule changes without a server restart; these specific capabilities describe MT5 rather than every MT4 tool.

Delivery & Cross-functional Collaboration

My role connects product priorities and stakeholder needs with specialist engineering work. Plugin implementation remains a technical team responsibility within the product delivery process.

Outcomes & Product Value

The tools support brokerage control over execution behavior and operating rules, with an emphasis on applying changes while preserving operational continuity.
