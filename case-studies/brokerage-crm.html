Product overview

A brokerage CRM ecosystem connecting administration, client onboarding, KYC, wallets, support, account workflows and reporting. The supplied artifacts cover desktop admin, trader-room and native mobile touchpoints.

Business problem

The TopFast presentation describes brokerage operations fragmented across systems, teams and integrations. My CV describes replacing the brokerage CRM while introducing a more structured product process with the existing delivery teams.

Target users

Brokerage operations and back-office teams, traders, introducing brokers and ProTraders. These roles are visible in the supplied product material; user research segments and interview counts are not disclosed.

My product role

Product Manager at Top Fast Technology, May 2023–May 2024; CPO from May 2024. I led discovery, redesign and delivery of the replacement CRM and remain hands-on in the native mobile CRM’s scope and UX. Designers, .NET/React engineers, QA and a Flutter developer contributed their respective disciplines.

Product strategy and discovery

Introduced structured discovery, stakeholder-validated Figma prototypes, prioritized backlogs, RACI responsibilities, Event Storming and Scrum ceremonies. The CV describes usability testing, Hotjar and direct customer feedback to refine requirements and design-to-engineering handoffs.

Main capabilities

Customer onboarding and KYC; wallets and requests; support and account workflows; IB, social-trading and risk connectivity; roles, permissions and reporting. These capabilities are documented in the supplied presentation and designs.

Key workflows

Administration: monitor customer and trading activity, then review operational requests. Trader room: access accounts, wallets and support actions. Mobile: move between Trader, IB and ProTrader contexts, use quick actions and inspect analytics.

Delivery and technical collaboration

Worked with the existing .NET/React engineering and QA teams and collaborated on an ABP-based microservices transition. Embedded QA earlier in the lifecycle. For the native mobile CRM, shaped scope and UX while working closely with a Flutter developer through build and iteration.

Outcomes supported by evidence

The supplied CV reports delivery of the replacement CRM in under eight months using the existing teams, with deployment across multiple brokerages. This is CV-reported delivery evidence; screenshots establish design context, not adoption or performance metrics.
