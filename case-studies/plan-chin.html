Product Overview

Plan Chin is a travel-expense and budget-sharing application for groups planning trips and managing shared costs.

Users & Product Experience

Users can budget for a trip, record shared expenses, split travel costs and calculate each person’s contributions and balance.

Product Context

Plan Chin is part of the SJI team’s product work. I include it alongside my enterprise product experience while keeping this consumer application separate from internal HR and service portals.
