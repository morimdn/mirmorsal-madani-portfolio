Product overview

A social-trading product connecting ProTrader discovery, performance context and subscription controls for investors.

Business problem

Investors need understandable performance information and allocation controls when choosing whom to follow. The presentation describes discovery, allocation, subscription models and governance as separate parts of the journey.

Target users

Investors/followers, ProTraders and brokerage teams managing qualification and subscription rules.

My product role

My CV establishes CPO responsibility for product strategy, roadmaps, prioritization and cross-functional teams across this portfolio. Product designers and engineers contributed the design and implementation work. The supplied UI artifacts are not presented as work I designed or coded alone.

Product strategy and discovery

The supplied product strategy emphasizes performance transparency, follower controls and risk settings. Specific research, prioritization and usability decisions attributable to me are pending confirmation.

Main capabilities

ProTrader discovery and filtering; watchlists; return and performance views; account selection; subscription terms; risk and trade-volume settings; renewal controls.

Key workflows

Discover ProTraders, inspect performance and subscription details, select an account, review allocation and risk settings, and confirm following. The provided follow dialog makes renewal and consent controls explicit.

Delivery and technical collaboration
