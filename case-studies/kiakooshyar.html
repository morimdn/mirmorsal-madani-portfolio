Kiakooshyar HR PWA

An independently designed and built HR PWA, with an MVP delivered and in use by the client HR team.

Company / contextIndependent Contract Product
My roleContract Product Lead & Hands-on Builder
OverviewMy roleCapabilitiesProduct value

Product Overview

An HR management super app delivered as a progressive web application. It covers onboarding, performance reviews, 360-degree evaluation and employee leaderboards.

Business Problem

The client needed a working HR product connecting related employee workflows in one application.

Target Users

The client’s HR team and employees participating in onboarding, review and feedback workflows.

My Role & Ownership

I independently led stakeholder discovery, requirements engineering, workflow sketching and Figma design, then built the PWA using AI-assisted development.

Product Discovery & Strategy

I translated stakeholder needs into workflow sketches and Figma designs, then defined the MVP scope. The next iteration is planned around consolidated feedback from the HR team using the product.

Key Product Decisions & Capabilities

Onboarding, performance reviews, 360-degree evaluation and employee leaderboards form the initial product scope. The MVP connects these HR workflows within one PWA.

Delivery & Cross-functional Collaboration

I took the product from discovery and design through hands-on implementation and delivery to the client HR team. AI-assisted development supported the build process, while I retained responsibility for the product and implementation.

Outcomes & Product Value

A working MVP was delivered and is in use by the client’s HR team. Further iterations will build on their consolidated feedback.
