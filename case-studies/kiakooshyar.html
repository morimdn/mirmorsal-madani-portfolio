Product overview

An independent HR management super app built as a PWA. The CV reports a working MVP delivered to and in use by the client HR team.

Business problem

Support HR workflows spanning onboarding, performance reviews, 360-degree evaluation and employee leaderboards in one product.

The client HR team and employees participating in HR workflows. Client identity and user counts are not disclosed.

Discovery and workflow definition were followed by Figma design and an MVP. Further iterations are planned after the HR team consolidates feedback, according to the CV.
